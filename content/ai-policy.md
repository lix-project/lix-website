---
title: "AI Policy"
description: "Our stance on AI/LLMs"
date: "2026-07-09"
author: "Lix Team"
---

The Lix Project does not allow any contributions which are believed to include AI generated content, or to be derived from AI generated content.

Using generative AI tooling in a way that does not result in creative AI generated content appearing in the contributed work is tolerated. This includes, for example:

- AI helping summarize a documentation page to assist in developing a feature.
- AI-based assistive technology (e.g. screen reader, speech to text) leaving full creative control to the author.
- AI assisting as part of static analysis or debugging workflows.

Contributions which are openly or obviously in violation of this AI Usage Policy will be rejected. In doubt or in case of repeated infractions, please contact the Lix Coreteam.

Infractions to this policy will lead to consequences such as a warning or a suspension from the Lix project (temporary or permanent).


Note: This AI-Policy is a modified version of the [AFNix AI-Policy](https://docs.afnix.fr/policies/ai.html).
